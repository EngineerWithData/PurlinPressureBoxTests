# Decisions on the open questions

The review raised 100 questions that the extracted tables could not answer on their own. Each was answered by Cris Moen after checking the report PDF. This page lists every question with the decision and the date it was made; it is Appendix B of the [assembly report](report/report.pdf). The same records are in [`review/open_questions.csv`](../review/open_questions.csv). Questions on reports later excluded from the database are included, because their answers led to the exclusions. Page numbers are PDF pages unless stated otherwise.
